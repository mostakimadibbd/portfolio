01 — About

Engineering intelligence into products people trust

I work at the seam between research and production — where a promising model has to become a reliable service with latency budgets, cost controls and monitoring.

{{PROFILE_SUMMARY}}

Over the last five years I have designed and shipped machine-learning systems across document intelligence, recommendation, forecasting and computer vision. My work starts with the data: I build clean, versioned datasets and evaluation harnesses first, because a metric you cannot trust is indistinguishable from noise.

On the engineering side I own the whole path to production — containerised training jobs, CI gates that block regressions, low-latency inference services and dashboards that tell me when a model is quietly drifting. I write TypeScript and Python daily, and I care about the details that make software pleasant to use: keyboard navigation, meaningful error messages, and interfaces that stay responsive on a mid-range phone.

I care about the teams behind the systems too: writing code that a colleague can pick up on a Tuesday, documenting the decisions that are hard to reverse, and leaving the codebase calmer than I found it.
